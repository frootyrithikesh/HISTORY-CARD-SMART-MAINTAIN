AMC Email Reminders

Expiry reminders are calculated only from the exact AMC expiry date entered in each contract.

Automatic reminder delivery

The Google Apps Script daily trigger sends reminders to all four nominated recipients: rithikeshrathi@bharatpetroleum.in, gauravmadan@bharatpetroleum.in, patilvishalravi@bharatpetroleum.in atharavsanjayka@bharatpetroleum.in and frootyrithikesh@gmail.com.

After expiry, a reminder is sent every day: −1, −2, −3, −4 days overdue, and daily thereafter.

The Apps Script trigger checks the shared AMC register daily at approximately 8:00 AM India time. All four recipients receive one common reminder email when a contract reaches a reminder date. Save AMC changes and confirm cloud sync before relying on reminders.
